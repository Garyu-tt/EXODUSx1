# R07: selected EL1 system state and two-stage translation

QEMU-only reference, based on R06. Saves/restores twelve additional EL1 registers (TTBR0/1, TCR, MAIR, AMAIR, VBAR, SCTLR, CPACR, ELR, SPSR, ESR, FAR). Context 960 bytes. Separate stage-1 root IPAs, ASIDs, stage-2 roots, VMIDs and backing pages; stage-1 tables read-only in stage-2. Both trusted EL1 guests execute at VA 0x1000 and use private data at VA 0x2000; require actual stage-1 translation plus stage-2. Verify TTBR0, M bit, VBAR, ELR_EL1 and FAR_EL1, existing full SIMD patterns and 128 cooperative yields. Selected register coverage only, not a complete guest kernel or proof of arbitrary EL1 isolation. No guest-created mappings, timer integration, virtual GIC, PMU/debug, SMP, physical Switch or Horizon/FreeBSD boot. Fresh CI required. Refs #103 #105 #355 #357.
